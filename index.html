Engineer · Filmmaker · Tinkerer

Building things. Collecting stories. Figuring out life.

I’m Jacky. In third grade I decided I would become an inventor; it took me a few years to learn the job title is “engineer.” I’ve been making videos for about as long. Right now I’m building Saga, an AI director that turns everyone’s footage from a shared day into one film. Before that, I spent four years at Amazon building AI systems for messy, real-world documents.

Now

Saga

“What’s your story?”

A birthday, a trip, a Sunday that turned into something. Everyone who was there adds their clips, and Saga finds the moments and the chapters and edits them into one finished film. Anyone can watch with a link.

Visit saga.you Saga on the App Store

The question underneath it

How can a machine tell which moments matter to the people who were there, and not just which ones look good?

I’m building it end to end: the multi-agent editing pipeline, an evaluation lab for creative judgment, and the iOS app. I think it’s the first project that uses everything on this page at once: engineering, film, and people.

The light

Hold the heart.

I’ve built a few LED pieces. This is the interactive one: a heart big enough to climb, built with Moon Landing Camp, outlined in addressable LEDs, with a touch sensor. Left alone, it twinkles through the rainbow. Touch it and a warm charge climbs from the tip of the heart to the top. Keep holding on and it starts to beat. Let go, and it drains away.

  • IdleRainbow twinkle
  • ChargingWarm light climbs the heart
  • FullA red heartbeat
  • ReleaseThe charge drains back down

This is a simplified browser re-creation of the piece’s behavior with 300 LEDs, debounce included: a touch only counts after it’s been steady for a moment.

Principles

How I see and approach life and the world.

First principles thinking

Understanding the foundations on top of which assumptions are made is important for approaching anything in life. Without direction, our actions could even be harmful rather than helpful. Think before acting.

Stoicism & openness

We are in control of our destiny. Our lives are filled with decisions that we can either make thoughtfully, or go along with, without ownership of it. Bad things happen. Own how we respond to and grow from them.

Humility & awareness: sonder

Everyone is living a complex life with their own set of challenges and goals. Respect that. Assume better of people even when we don’t believe they deserve it.
